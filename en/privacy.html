Privacy

This local version is intended for review and testing. Before collecting public requests, the owner must identify the data controller, processing grounds, retention periods and procedures for access and deletion requests.

Form data

The form asks for a name, phone number, email address, client type and task description. These details are sent through Formspree to process the request and notify the manager. Submitting the form indicates consent to this transfer. Do not enter passwords, access keys or other secrets.

Transfer and storage

The form sends data to the Formspree endpoint configured for this project. Processing and storage by the service are governed by Formspree’s terms and privacy policy. The project owner should confirm retention periods and deletion procedures in the service settings.
