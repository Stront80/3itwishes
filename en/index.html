Any questions?

What counts as one task?

A task is an agreed scope of work estimated at up to 8 hours combined across all specialists. We discuss scope and feasibility before starting.

When does the subscription start?

It starts upon activation agreed with your manager. It lasts 30 calendar days and ends at the same time of day 30 days later.

Can I apply on behalf of a business?

Yes. We consider requests from both individuals and businesses.

What if a task takes more than eight hours?

Describe it in the form. Before starting, we discuss the scope and whether it fits the subscription. Submitting a request does not activate a subscription.
